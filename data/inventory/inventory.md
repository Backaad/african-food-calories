## How to read this table

- **Commons**: files in the dish's Wikimedia Commons categories whose own licence is CC0, public domain, CC BY or CC BY-SA, and that are JPEG/PNG/WebP.
- **Commons search**: extra open-licence files whose *title* contains the dish name, for dishes without a good category. Not curated: upper bound only.
- **Rejected**: files in the same categories or searches that were dropped (NC/ND/unknown licence, or not a photo format).
- **Openverse**: open-licence search hits outside Wikimedia. Keyword matches, *not reviewed*: many are not the dish. Upper bound only. Anonymous searches stop at 240, shown as ≥240.
- **Status** uses the reliable count (Commons + approved datasets): ≥ 150 → model, ≥ 20 → few-shot test, otherwise manual entry. *If the upper bound holds* adds Commons search and Openverse hits.
- No count here has been reviewed by a person yet. "Usable" photos (right dish, real photo, no duplicates) will be fewer.
